Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions carriers ask us most — answered straight.

What does HonorLine do?

We are your dispatch and back office. We search the load boards for freight that fits your truck and your lanes, negotiate the rate with the broker, send you the load for approval, and — once you say yes — handle the rate confirmation, carrier paperwork, check calls, and invoicing behind the run. You drive; we handle the rest.

What do I need to get started?

You need your own operating authority — an active MC/DOT number — along with your truck and equipment, insurance, and a signed carrier agreement with us. We dispatch carriers under their own authority; we don’t provide trucks, trailers, or authority. Fill out the Carrier Setup form below and we’ll walk you through the rest.

How is the fee decided?

The dispatch fee is agreed in writing in your carrier agreement before any load is booked — no surprises and no changes after the fact. You’ll know exactly what dispatch costs before your first load ever rolls.

How do I get paid?

You are paid by the broker or shipper for the loads you haul — the freight money is yours. We prepare and send your invoices and paperwork promptly after each delivery so your billing goes out complete, and we follow up on the details as agreed in your carrier agreement.

Do you book loads without my approval?

Never. Every load — rate, pickup, destination, and timing — comes to you first, and nothing is booked without your yes. There is no forced dispatch at HonorLine: if a load doesn’t work for you, we pass on it and keep searching.

Privacy Policy

What we collect. When you contact us or fill out the Carrier Setup form, we receive the details you send us: your name or company name, MC/DOT number, truck and equipment type, preferred lanes, email address, and phone number, along with the contents of your emails to us.

How we use it. We use this information only to respond to you, set up your carrier profile, dispatch your truck, and handle the paperwork and communication involved in that work. That includes sharing the carrier details brokers need to see in setup packets and rate confirmations for loads you approve.

What we don’t do. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not share it for marketing. This website has no tracking cookies and no sign-up accounts — the setup form simply opens an email in your own Gmail.

Questions. To ask about your information, or to have it removed from our records, email us at info@honorlinedispatch.com.

Terms of Service

Our service. HonorLine Dispatch provides dispatch and back-office support to carriers operating under their own MC/DOT authority. We search for and negotiate loads on your behalf; every load is booked only with your approval. There is no forced dispatch.

Fees. The dispatch fee is agreed in writing in each carrier agreement before any load is booked. The fee in your signed agreement is the fee that applies — it is not changed on a load that has already been booked.

Payment. Freight charges for loads you haul are paid to you by the broker or shipper. Any payment terms, invoicing steps, or timing connected with our dispatch service apply as agreed in your carrier agreement.

Cancellation. Either side may end the working relationship as agreed in your carrier agreement. Loads already booked and confirmed before cancellation are handled as agreed in your carrier agreement.

Your responsibilities. You remain responsible for your authority, insurance, equipment, safety compliance, and for running every load you approve. If anything here ever differs from your signed carrier agreement, the signed agreement controls.
