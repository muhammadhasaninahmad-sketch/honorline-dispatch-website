What We Do

Dispatch Built for Box Trucks

Box truck freight is its own market — retail deliveries, LTL, and final-mile runs that big-trailer dispatchers overlook. This is the work we do for box truck carriers every day.

Daily Load Search

We search the load boards daily for freight that fits a 26-ft box truck — local and regional runs, retail deliveries, LTL, and final-mile work — matched to your lanes and your schedule.

Rate Negotiation

We negotiate the rate with the broker and look at the whole run — loaded miles and empty miles — before a load ever comes to you. No load is brought to you at a rate we would not run ourselves.

Paperwork & Check Calls

Rate confirmations, carrier setup packets, PODs, and invoicing — handled. We make the check calls to brokers and shippers so you do not have to.

Parking & Rest Planning

Early pickup the next morning? We help you line up safe parking near the pickup and the delivery the night before, so you start the run rested instead of hunting for a spot.

Questions

Box Truck Dispatch — Frequently Asked Questions

The questions box truck carriers ask us most — answered straight.

How much does box truck dispatch cost?

The dispatch fee is agreed in writing in your carrier agreement before any load is booked — no surprises and no changes after the fact. You’ll know exactly what dispatch costs before your first load ever rolls.

What do I need to get started?

You need your own operating authority (MC number) and your box truck — we do not provide trucks, trailers, or authority; we are your dispatch and back office. Fill out the Carrier Setup form on our home page and we’ll walk you through the rest.

What size box trucks do you dispatch?

26-foot box trucks and other straight box trucks, running local and regional freight — retail deliveries, LTL, and final-mile runs. If you run a different size, ask us — if the freight fits your truck, we will search for it.

Do you book loads without my approval?

Never. Every load comes to you first, and every fee is settled before it is charged. A load is booked only when you say yes — your truck, your call.
